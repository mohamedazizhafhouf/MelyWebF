Zakat estimator

Enter zakatable assets and deductible debts in one currency (your choice). Niṣāb uses gold weight from defaults × your gold price per gram. This is a simplified educational tool—not a religious ruling. Numbers update instantly in your browser (no server round-trip).

Not a fatwa. Zakat depends on many details (asset types, debt timing, schools of thought). Confirm with a qualified scholar or trusted charity before paying. If one lunar year (ḥawl) has not passed on your zakatable wealth, you do not owe Zakat yet; the calculator still shows a hypothetical 2.5% figure for planning.
